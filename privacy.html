Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

At OmniResume, accessible from our website, safeguarding your privacy is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by OmniResume and how we use it.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing

All resume generation, formatting, and text processing on OmniResume occur strictly within your browser's local memory (RAM). We do not upload, store, or log your personal resume data, employment history, or cover letters on any remote servers.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
